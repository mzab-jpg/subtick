# Tangent logo concepts — review only

These are design mockups. They are **not connected to the application**, `app.json`, Android adaptive icon assets, splash screen, or any in-app component.

Open each SVG in VS Code or a browser:

### Initial directions

1. `01-editorial-t.svg` — simple typographic mark. Safest at small sizes.
2. `02-tangent-line.svg` — line/circle idea based on the word “tangent”; most distinctive and the best candidate for a subtle loading animation.
3. `03-direction-mark.svg` — a flat black/off-white version of the existing upward-chevron/direction icon.

### T-first explorations

4. `04-typographic-tangent.svg` — bold T with a circular interruption at its discovery point.
5. `05-split-t.svg` — crossbar and stem deliberately separated; the gap is the mark.
6. `06-bookmark-t.svg` — a T whose stem finishes as a bookmark.
7. `07-discovery-spark-t.svg` — a solid T with one controlled red discovery spark.
8. `08-impossible-t.svg` — a geometric T that weaves through itself.

### Modern utility explorations

9. `09-folded-tile.svg` — a single useful card with a deliberately folded corner.
10. `10-offset-cards.svg` — three offset content cards; the top selected item rises forward.
11. `11-modular-t.svg` — simple blocks that create a subtle T only through their arrangement.
12. `12-open-aperture.svg` — an open frame/window with one purposeful gap.

Each sheet previews the same concept as a deliberately large app icon test, startup screen, loading mark, and dark-mode note.

## What has deliberately not changed

- `assets/` remains untouched.
- `app.json` remains untouched.
- No icon, splash, startup, or loading behaviour in the app has changed.
- No APK rebuild is needed to inspect these mockups.

## Suggested review questions

- Does it look recognisable at the small loading-mark size?
- Does it fit the calm editorial Reader and Dashboard, rather than looking like a generic news app?
- Does it still look like Tangent if the red dot is removed for Android’s monochrome themed icon?
