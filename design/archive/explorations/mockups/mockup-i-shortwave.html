How Shortwave maps to Tangent

  • LCD hero = position-0 ranked article. Side frequencies = queue positions 1–2. SCAN/RANDOM = Discover/Shuffle callables.
  • VU meters = stats pill (≤3 metrics, placeholder until verified).
  • Signal lock reader = WebView + oscilloscope-as-progress-bar (scroll depth) + HUD strip (close/like/save).
  • Preset BOOST/NEUTRAL/CUT = 3-state category chips updating catLatent weights.
  • Tape log / Vault = History (seen metadata) / Saved Reads (offline HTML).
  • Back panel screws = Settings card groups; no new capabilities invented.
